## The Short Version

- This website has no forms and no accounts. We don't ask for your details here.
- If you agree, we use Google Analytics to count visits so we know what's useful. If you say no, it never loads.
- We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. Ever.

## What We Collect

**When you browse this site.** If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 records things like which pages were viewed, roughly where in the world the visit came from, the type of device and browser, and how you arrived (for example, from a Facebook post). It doesn't tell us who you are. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4.

**When you call or email us.** We keep what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address and details about your business, so we can reply and, if we work together, deliver our services.

## The Analytics Banner

The first time you visit, a small banner asks whether we may count your visit. Until you choose, no analytics code runs. Your choice is saved in your browser so we don't keep asking. To change your mind, clear this site's data in your browser settings and the banner will ask again.

## How We Use Information

- To answer your calls and emails.
- To provide and improve our services if you become a client.
- To understand, in aggregate, which pages are helpful so we can make the site better.

## Who Else Sees It

- **Google** processes analytics data on our behalf if you accept analytics. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing.
- **GitHub** hosts this website and, like any web host, may keep standard server logs for security.
- Otherwise, we only share information if the law requires it.

## Your Choices

You can decline analytics, ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call or email us and we'll handle it promptly.

## Children

This site is meant for adults running businesses and campaigns. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

## Questions

Call or email us using the details at the bottom of this page. A real person will answer.
